# Bhavya Shah
### Quantitative Finance | Financial Modelling | Applied Machine Learning

I build computational projects at the intersection of quantitative finance, financial engineering, stochastic modelling, and machine learning.

My current interests centre on derivatives valuation, volatility and portfolio risk, fixed-income modelling, systematic investment strategies, and the careful application of machine learning to financial problems.

## Research and Technical Interests

- **Derivatives and stochastic modelling:** option pricing, Monte Carlo methods, Brownian motion, stochastic volatility, short-rate models.
- **Portfolio research:** portfolio analytics, factor models, PCA-based statistical arbitrage, risk-adjusted performance.
- **Risk management:** Value at Risk, filtered historical simulation, GARCH-family volatility models, fixed-income revaluation.
- **Fixed income:** yield-curve interpolation, SOFR curve construction, swaps, floating-rate instruments.
- **Financial machine learning:** regression, supervised learning, neural-network pricing surrogates, computational benchmarking.

## Approach to Research

I aim to make quantitative work transparent and reproducible. I place emphasis on documenting assumptions, separating training and test periods, evaluating out-of-sample behaviour, accounting for model limitations, and distinguishing statistical performance from economic usefulness.

Most repositories are independent learning and research implementations. Their READMEs describe the current scope and identify areas requiring further validation. Results should be interpreted within their stated assumptions.
